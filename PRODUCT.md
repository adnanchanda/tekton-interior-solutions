## Platform

## Users

- Admin, facility and procurement managers at companies in and around Pune who are fitting out or expanding an office and need workstations, chairs, storage and reception furniture made and installed. (inferred)
- Architects, interior designers and fit-out contractors looking for a manufacturer to build to their drawings. (inferred)
- Pune homeowners who want modular wardrobes, kitchens and bedroom furniture made to measure. (inferred from the owner's statement that the business makes residential furniture)

Most arrive on a phone from Google Search, Google Maps or an AI assistant, compare two or three vendors, and want to call or WhatsApp rather than fill in a long form.

## Product Purpose

The website for Tekton Interior Solutions, a furniture manufacturer in Pune. It must get the business found for furniture-manufacturer searches in Pune, answer the questions buyers ask before they call, and turn a visit into a phone call, a WhatsApp message or a quote request. Success is measured in logged enquiries, not traffic.

## Positioning

Tekton makes the furniture in its own factory in Yewalewadi, Pune and installs it. It is a manufacturer, not a showroom or a reseller, and it covers both office and residential work.

## Operating Context

Buyers evaluate from floor plans, seat counts, laminate shade cards, BOQs and per-seat quotations. Orders are made to measure and delivered and installed on site.
